# Webby Wahine project strategy · Ariel Bunao · Option B

## Business interpretation

Ariel works in acrylic on canvas and paints people, places, and moments of Hawaiʻi. She wants her first website to introduce her art, present a gallery, and lead to sales. Her stated audience loves the islands and the lifestyle. The preview's primary conversion is a specific artwork inquiry; secondary conversions are general art and print inquiries. Her submitted paintings and process photos are the strongest supplied trust signals. The business name, physical location, sales reach, artist history, and differentiators have not been confirmed.

## User journey

A visitor meets Ariel and sees an actual painting immediately, then browses a small exhibition of distinct works, sees Ariel creating, and asks about a piece. The largest purchasing barrier is missing titles, dimensions, prices, editions, and availability; the preview does not pretend these are known. It invites a direct conversation. Ariel is comfortable having Webby Wahine update the portfolio quarterly, so this concept does not need a content manager or online checkout at launch.

## Architecture

Single-page navigation: Paintings → The artist → Inquire. Sections: dramatic artist introduction, featured floral work, six-work collection, artist and process, inquiry. The footer closes with her provisional name. No empty FAQ, manufactured testimonials, or shopping cart.

## Search and content

Likely search intent centers on Ariel's name and acrylic paintings connected with Hawaiʻi. Natural secondary topics include original paintings, prints, the subjects of individual works, and her process. The title and description reflect actual supplied information. A future public site should contain confirmed artwork names, dimensions, medium, availability, clean photographs, a fuller artist bio, and location or fulfillment information if Ariel wants it published. A production domain will allow a canonical URL, sitemap, social metadata, and appropriate robots rules. This GitHub concept preview uses `noindex` without blocking crawling in `robots.txt`.

## Pre-launch status

- **Ready for concept review:** original design, curated supplied images, responsive code, inquiry flow.
- **Needs Ariel:** name/brand preference, bio, image rights, public contact preference, artwork metadata, current availability, print and sale process.
- **Recommended:** dedicated straight-on photos of finished paintings and a later ecommerce assessment if stock or sales volume grows.
- **Blocking a public business launch:** approved copy/assets, accurate purchasing details, production domain and search configuration, completed device and accessibility QA.
